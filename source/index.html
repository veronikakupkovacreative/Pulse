03 / DESCRIPTIONFORMAT CONTROL

Format
Converter.

A practical tool for quickly changing formats and adjusting export settings for your audio files directly within the application interface.

How to use:

  1. Have your loaded and edited track ready in the workspace.
  2. Choose your desired output format and quality settings.
  3. Run the conversion and download the finished file to your device.
03 / DESCRIPTIONHARMONIC ANALYSIS

Key
Finder.

Harmonic analysis that estimates the musical key of a track (including Camelot notation) to help you with composition, mixing, and creating harmonic transitions.

How to use:

  1. Load your track into the main editor.
  2. Start the key analysis in the Key Finder tab.
  3. Verify the estimated key by ear based on the displayed results.
03 / DESCRIPTIONPRECISION EDIT

Loop
Cutter.

A tool designed to precisely cut a musical loop based on selected rhythmic divisions (e.g., 4/4).

How to use:

  1. Mark the desired section on the waveform using markers.
  2. Adjust the duration according to the selected rhythmic division.
  3. Listen to and check the created loop before exporting.
03 / DESCRIPTIONREVERSE AUDIO

Audio
Reverse.

Instantly reverses audio playback direction (backward) while maintaining the exact same duration and original sample rate.

How to use:

  1. Load the audio track into the editor.
  2. Activate the Audio Reverse function for the entire track or a selected section.
  3. Listen to the result and save it.
03 / DESCRIPTIONCLEAN EDITING

Silence
Trimmer.

Automatically detects and trims silent areas, pauses, or noise at the very beginning or end of a recording.

How to use:

  1. Load audio into the workspace.
  2. Check the detected trim boundaries and adjust sensitivity.
  3. Confirm silence removal for a clean start and end.
03 / DESCRIPTIONTIME & TEMPO

BPM
Calculator.

Converts the current tempo (BPM) into precise time values in milliseconds for individual note lengths, which is useful when setting effects like delay.

How to use:

  1. Enter the BPM value or let it load from the current project.
  2. Browse the clear table of time values.
  3. Click any value to easily copy it to your clipboard.
03 / DESCRIPTIONWSOLA PROCESSING

Pitch &
Time.

Allows you to change track tempo or shift pitch using WSOLA-based time processing and pitch shifting while aiming to preserve natural playback.

How to use:

  1. Load audio and set the desired target BPM or pitch shift.
  2. Monitor the changes between the original and target states.
  3. Apply processing and check the result by playing it back.
03 / DESCRIPTIONBASS TUNING

808
Tuner.

A specialized tool for detecting fundamental frequencies and precisely tuning low-frequency 808 bass samples.

How to use:

  1. Upload a clean, sustained 808 sample.
  2. Run analysis from the playhead position (with an option to skip the sample attack).
  3. Check the frequency in Hz and cents and perform manual fine-tuning if needed.
03 / DESCRIPTIONTRANSIENT EDITING

Sample
Slicer.

Analyzes transients in an audio file and automatically slices the sample into smaller, independently usable parts.

How to use:

  1. Load audio into the editor.
  2. Run transient detection.
  3. Adjust slice points and work with individual sample sections.
03 / DESCRIPTIONLOUDNESS CONTROL

AI
Normalizer.

Provides full control over output gain and loudness normalization of recorded audio.

How to use:

  1. Load the audio track into the editor.
  2. Select the desired level or normalization type.
  3. Apply changes for optimal signal level without clipping.
03 / DESCRIPTION20-BAND EQ

Parametric
Equalizer.

An advanced local DSP equalizer with a selectable number of bands (3 to 20) for detailed frequency adjustments from 20 Hz to 20 kHz.

How to use:

  1. Select the desired number of bands and adjust the EQ curve directly under the waveform.
  2. Click a specific band on the curve for fine-tuning parameters.
  3. Use the available sound presets for a quick start.
03 / DESCRIPTIONNOISE REDUCTION

Spectral
Denoise.

An advanced tool featuring a 2048-point FFT for cleaning unwanted noise from recordings by learning a noise profile.

How to use:

  1. Use IN/OUT markers on the waveform to select a short section containing only background noise (minimum 120 ms).
  2. Save the noise fingerprint.
  3. Apply denoising to the entire stereo track and check the result.
03 / DESCRIPTIONTONAL CORRECTION & COLOR

AI Studio
Mix.

A comprehensive workspace to shape your mix. It offers either measured tonal correction and harmonic coloration for a finished stereo file, or the ability to mix 2 to 8 aligned stems.

How to use:

  1. Choose your input mode (Stereo mix or Multitrack stems).
  2. Adjust tonal character and saturation for stereo, or balance individual channel levels for multitrack.
  3. Optionally use local Demucs ONNX stem separation to split vocals, drums, bass, and other elements.
03 / DESCRIPTIONADAPTIVE DSP MASTERING

AI Studio
Master.

An adaptive DSP mastering tool that helps give your recording consistent loudness, balanced tonal characteristics, and clarity according to your chosen master character.

How to use:

  1. Load your finished mix into the workspace.
  2. Choose your preferred master character (e.g., Clean) and target loudness in LUFS.
  3. Review the final report (before/after comparison, true peak, stereo correlation) and export your finished work.
